Day 3 Sun 14 Jun — Waterfalls Day 💧💧💧 🏨 Bondowoso
06:00🍳
Early breakfast · move fast
Three spots today plus a 4.5hr drive tonight. Pace matters.
07:00💎
Kapas Biru Waterfall ⭐
Blue-green water that looks fake. Swim if you want. ~1 hr.
08:45🏔️
Teras Semeru Viewpoint ⭐
Semeru (3,676m), Java's highest volcano, actively erupting. The photo from here is unfair. ~1 hr.
10:15🌊
Tumpak Sewu Waterfall ⭐⭐⭐
120m wide curtain waterfall. Bamboo ladder descent — wet, steep, borderline unhinged. The view at the bottom hits completely different. ~3 hrs.
13:15🧴
Done · change into dry set · lunch
Dry clothes from the bag. Long drive ahead.
14:45🚗
Drive → Bondowoso (~4.5 hrs)
Must arrive before 20:00. Sleep window: tonight 20:00–23:30.
19:15🏨
Check-in Bondowoso · eat · sleep immediately
Checkout is at 23:30. You have 4 hours. Use them.
⚠️ Heaviest day of the trip. Eat well, drink water, move efficiently.

Day 4 Mon 15 Jun — Kawah Ijen 🔵 🏨 Surabaya
23:30🌙
Checkout · all layers on before leaving
Cold outside. Coffee if you have it. Gas mask and headlamp ready.
00:00🚗
Transfer to Paltuding Basecamp
~1.5hr drive · sleep in the van
02:00🥾
Start trekking Kawah Ijen
3km uphill · ~2hrs · headlamp + gas mask on · don't fall behind
04:30🔵
Blue Fire at the crater ⭐⭐⭐
Actual blue flames from burning sulfur gas. One of two spots on earth. Before dawn only. This is why we came.
05:30🌅

Turquoise lake, most acidic in the world. The colour is unreal. Sulfur gas is intense — mask stays on.
07:30⬇️
Descend · back to basecamp
Watch the trail down — rocky and steep. Trekking pole helps.
09:30🍳
Breakfast · change clothes · debrief
Change into comfortable clothes before the long drive home.
10:30🚗
Drive → Surabaya (~5 hrs)
Trip is basically done. Sleep the whole way.
15:30🏨
Check-in Surabaya · rest · dinner
Last night. Celebrate.
🎉 That's it. Blue fire seen. Trip complete.
