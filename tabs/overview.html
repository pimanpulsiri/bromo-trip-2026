East Java straight up hits different. We're starting with Madakaripura — a hidden canyon where water comes at you from everywhere. You will get soaked. That's the whole point. Then it's a 2-hour climb up to Bromo to sleep before the 3am Jeep ride to one of the best sunrises on earth.

Day three is Tumpak Sewu, which is as unhinged as it sounds — 120 meters of curtain waterfall with a bamboo ladder descent that's wet and steep and kind of terrifying. The view at the bottom makes you forget all of it. We cap the whole trip with Kawah Ijen's blue fire — one of two places on earth where it exists, and it only shows before sunrise. So we go at midnight. That's just how it is.
